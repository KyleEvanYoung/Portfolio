Kyle Young – 3D Artist | Game Developer | Author

I’m a passionate 3D artist with experience working on a range of game projects, from quick development cycles to more ambitious titles. Most of the games I’ve contributed to were built within a few weeks, but my most notable project is Short Staffed — a four-player party game with a free demo available. As Lead 3D Artist, I was responsible for creating all character models and designing half of the game’s maps and assets.

Beyond game development, I’ve participated in multiple rendering challenges, constantly pushing my creative and technical skills.

I'm also the author of Paradox: The Traveller, a novel I've long wanted to write. It’s a time-travel story that explores moral ambiguity, leadership, sacrifice, and the weight of destiny — all set against the tension of a ticking clock.

Paradox: The Traveller

In a fusion of destinies and mystical quests, Noah, known as ‘The Traveler,’ embarks on a perilous journey through time in search of magical elements essential for mastering the forces of creation. Alongside him, Joshua, an assassin from the future, grapples with his broken past and the haunting memories that threaten to overshadow his lethal skills. Meanwhile, Edward confronts the loss of his magical prowess and navigates his new existence as a dragon, a form both powerful and alien to him. These three extraordinary characters, each dealing with their own set of challenges, find their fates intertwined as they navigate the complexities of loyalty, power, and identity in a multiverse orchestrated by unseen forces. Together, they face an uncertain future, their paths marked by trials of magic, betrayal, and redemption.
