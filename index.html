Ever since I was a child, I’ve been enthralled by stories, myths, and legends that unfolded in movies, video games, and the imaginary adventures I’d create with friends and family. Inspired by these narratives, I started to craft my own tales during quiet moments, each character born from both joyous and challenging times. These stories have always offered me solace, especially during periods of boredom or restlessness. Yet, I found it daunting to share these creations in a way that truly resonated with others. That’s why I turned to writing, hoping to encapsulate just a sliver of the magic these tales have brought into my life. This story, although it navigates the grand themes of time travel and the monumental task of saving kingdoms and other worlds, is fundamentally about the human condition. It’s about individuals doing their best in seemingly impossible situations. Through this narrative, I want to remind readers that life is a mix of good times and bad times. What matters most isn’t what happens around us, but how we choose to react to these situations and treat the people we love. I hope that in reading this story, you find a touch of the joy, comfort, or peace that creating it has given me.
