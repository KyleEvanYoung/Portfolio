About The Story

I was given the themes Freedom and Confinement and the genre Mystery. I wanted to explore each idea in a unique way—for example, freedom as the ability to make choices or to go anywhere. That led me to imagine four characters, each seeking freedom in their own way:

A fairy who once had the freedom to fly anywhere but now cannot.

A knight who has lost the freedom to return home to his kingdom.

A wizard who once had the freedom to cast any spell but can no longer do so.

A witch trapped within her own kingdom.

At first, I wasn’t sure how to turn these ideas into a mystery. Then it hit me—what if one of the characters is lying or betrays the others? That twist could create suspense and make the story feel more like a true mystery.

Initially, these were just scattered ideas, not a complete story. But I kept thinking about how to tie everything together—and eventually, I figured it out.
