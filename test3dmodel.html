The model rotation is tied to page scroll.

As the user scrolls down the page, the GLB object rotates smoothly inside the 3D div. The center div stays visible at the top of the page on desktop because it is sticky.

The rig bone named Head follows the mouse.

The script looks through the loaded model for a bone exactly named Head. If found, it keeps the original bone pose and adds limited mouse-driven X/Y rotation on top, so the head follows the pointer without fully breaking the rig.

Transparent 3D background.

The Three.js scene and renderer are transparent, and the model div itself has no background fill. The model appears over whatever page design sits behind the canvas.
